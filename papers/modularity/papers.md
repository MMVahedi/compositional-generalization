## **2025**

### [Enabling Systematic Generalization in Abstract Spatial Reasoning through Meta-Learning for Compositionality](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.01445v1)

- **Year:** 2025
- **Conference/Venue:** Pre-print on arXiv (Apr 2025)
- **Institutions:** Philipp Mondorf, Shijia Zhou, Monica Riedler, Barbara Plank
- **Abstract:** Extends meta-learning for compositionality beyond language into abstract spatial reasoning. Introduces **SYGAR**, a dataset requiring models to generalize novel combinations of geometric transformations (e.g., translation + rotation). A transformer trained with meta-learning for compositionality achieves strong systematic generalization, outperforming large multimodal LLMs (GPT-4o, Gemini 2.0 Flash, o3-mini).
- **Keywords:** systematic generalization, meta-learning, spatial reasoning, compositionality, geometry, transformer

## **2024**

### [Sparse Mixture-of-Experts for Compositional Generalization: Empirical Evidence and Theoretical Foundations of Optimal Sparsity](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.13964v2)
- **Year:** 2024 / 2025
- **Conference/Venue:** Pre-print on arXiv (v2: June 2025)
- **Institutions:** University of Texas at Austin; Ohio State University; Cisco Research
- **Abstract:** Studies compositional generalization in **sparse mixture-of-experts (SMoE)** architectures. Finds that optimal expert activation scales with task complexity rather than being minimal. Provides a theoretical scaling law balancing approximation and estimation errors, validated on reasoning benchmarks (SRAVEN, SKILL-MIX).
- **Keywords:** mixture-of-experts, sparsity, compositional generalization, optimal routing, transformers, modularity

### [Attention as a Hypernetwork](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.05816)

- **Year:** 2024    
- **Conference/Venue:** Pre-print on arXiv (ICLR 2025 oral)
- **Institutions:** ETH Zürich; Google DeepMind
- **Abstract:** Reinterprets multi-head attention as a **hypernetwork**: attention scores form a latent code configuring a value network for each key-query pair. Nonlinear extensions improve compositional generalization in reasoning tasks such as symbolic Raven’s matrices.
- **Keywords:** transformer, hypernetwork, multi-head attention, compositional generalization, abstract reasoning, latent code

### [Block-Operations: Using Modular Routing to Improve Compositional Generalization](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.00508)

- **Year:** 2024    
- **Conference/Venue:** Pre-print on arXiv (v1: Aug 2024)
- **Institutions:** Saarland University, Germany
- **Abstract:** Introduces **block-operations** to enhance modular routing in neural networks. Proposes a **Multiplexer** component (SMFR architecture) that dynamically routes activation blocks, yielding perfect generalization in synthetic and permutation tasks.    
- **Keywords:** modular routing, block operations, compositional generalization, neural architecture, reasoning

## **2023**

### [Discovering Modular Solutions that Generalize Compositionally](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.15001v1)

- **Year:** 2023    
- **Conference/Venue:** Pre-print on arXiv (Dec 2023)
- **Institutions:** Meta-learning & Modularity Group
- **Abstract:** Analyzes when meta-learned **modular architectures** (e.g., hypernetworks) can discover compositional solutions that generalize beyond training distributions. Provides a teacher-student theory of modular identifiability and empirical validation showing hypernetworks outperform monolithic meta-learners like MAML.
- **Keywords:** modular architecture, hypernetwork, meta-learning, compositional generalization, identifiability

### [Human-like Systematic Generalization via Meta-Learning](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06668-3)

- **Year:** 2023    
- **Conference/Venue:** _Nature_ 623, 115–121
- **Institutions:** New York University; ICREA / Universitat Pompeu Fabra
- **Abstract:** Demonstrates that **meta-learning for compositionality (MLC)** enables neural networks to achieve human-like systematic generalization. Behavioral comparisons show MLC uniquely matches both human flexibility and systematicity, resolving a core critique of connectionist models.
- **Keywords:** systematic generalization, meta-learning, human-like reasoning, compositionality, cognitive modeling
